## 🔭 Research & Engineering Interests

- **Physics-informed Graph Neural Networks (PhyGNN)** for forward and inverse PDE problems
- **MeshGraphNets** and learned simulators for CFD / mesh-based physics
- **Graph learning on irregular domains** and spatiotemporal prediction
- **AI4Science**: scientific computing, PDE surrogates, physics-consistent deep learning
- **Large Language Models (LLM)**: RAG systems, retrieval, evaluation, deployment
- **LLM for Science**: LLM-assisted simulation, scientific agents and knowledge retrieval
- Also interested in **CV/ML engineering**, model deployment and practical AI systems
